Privacy Policy

This application uses TikTok Login Kit to allow users to sign in with their TikTok account.

The application may access basic profile information that the user has authorised TikTok to share.

Information is only accessed for the purpose of providing the application's features. We do not sell users' personal information or use it for advertising.

Users can revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through their TikTok settings.
